# Inspiration board study (inspo/, 35 images, 2026-10-09)

What the images are, grouped by what they share, and what the being does not do yet.

## What is in the folder
- **Fluorescence micrographs of tissue cross-sections**: plant stem and root sections (teal, blue-violet with orange and
  magenta organelles), intestinal villi (blue nuclei, orange membranes, red rim), a prostate tissue core (grey epithelial
  folds), a pancreatic islet / organoid (green membranes, blue nuclei, magenta), a tumour section.
- **Developmental biology**: blastula cleavage stages (zebrafish, amphibian, mammal, echinoderm, choanoflagellate), a
  sketched blastula / morula mosaic, a lung bud branching with a stress-coloured tip, ductal branching networks,
  plant meristems and SEM flower buds.
- **Pollen and diatoms**: SEM pollen grains (pores, furrows, reticulate nets, spines), a diatom.
- **Turing patterns on animals**: pufferfish skin (rings around the eye, stripes radiating from the mouth) next to their
  simulations; a radial green reaction-diffusion labyrinth.
- **Strange attractors as line art**: Lorenz trajectories, Clifford density (thin overlapping ellipses), Gumowski-Mira
  (stippled), Ikeda map iterations (dotted spiral), a chaos-competition attractor rendered as translucent pastel ribbons,
  a blue Lorenz ribbon, a colourful star icon from a symmetric map.
- **Circle inversion / Kleinian**: a sphere of nested circles (Apollonian), a golden-spiral Kleinian fractal (red, deep).
- **Graphic ornament**: Muisca/Tolima ceramic stamps (radial suns, concentric rings), a radial ink mandala, CPPN outputs.
- **Neuro**: DTI tractography of the brain (fibre streamlines coloured by direction).
- **Particle Lenia** screenshots (glowing coloured particle organisms) and a body made of spheres (purple organoid).
- **Mars topography** (Hydraotes Chaos) with a rainbow height colour map; a structural mesh diagram.

## The qualities they share (and the being lacks)
1. **Colour carries meaning.** In fluorescence each structure glows in its own channel on black: membranes one colour,
   nuclei another, organelles a third. Tractography colours by direction; topography colours by height. The being is
   nearly monochrome metal; its grown skin shapes height only, never colour.
2. **Nesting.** Rim, then compartments, then cells inside compartments, then nuclei inside cells (stem, root, villi,
   islet, Kleinian circles in circles). The skin generators are single-scale fields.
3. **A centre and radial order.** Almost everything is organised around a centre: sections, pollen, ornaments,
   pufferfish rings, the radial labyrinth. The genome has an axis and a symmetry, but the body is rarely seen down it.
4. **Luminous lines and filaments.** Attractors, Lorenz, tractography, ducts: thin bright trajectories that accumulate
   into translucent volumes. The attractors were used only as a height map, which throws this quality away.
5. **Pattern organised by anatomy.** On the pufferfish the stripes radiate from the mouth and ring the eye: the pattern
   follows the body's features. The skin ignores the body's features (lobes, openings, core).
6. **Seeing inside.** Sections, translucent ribbons and particle organisms show interiors. The body is mostly an opaque
   shell; only glass and the openings show the inside.
7. **Development as a process.** Cleavage, branching and growth over time. Only the cell family cleaves.
8. **Restraint on black.** One subject, one palette, dark ground, light only where structure is.
